Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This is a draft privacy notice for Music Edit Engine. It must be reviewed against the application's actual data handling, including the TikTok integration, before being used as the definitive public policy. Contact: 50018studio@gmail.com.

1. What the application does

Music Edit Engine is a desktop application intended to organize media, generate short-form videos, and support publishing to third-party social accounts when the relevant integration is enabled and authorized.

2. Information involved

Depending on the features used, the application may handle uploaded media, video-editing settings, publishing history, third-party account identifiers, and authorization credentials or tokens necessary for requested integrations. The specific information handled depends on the installed version and the permissions granted.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to perform user-requested functions, such as selecting and editing media, associating authorized accounts, preparing publishing jobs, and reporting their status. Third-party platform data should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis and disclosure.

4. Third-party services

If users choose to connect TikTok, YouTube, or another platform, their use of that service also falls under its own privacy policy and terms. Requests to those services are subject to the access permissions granted by the user.

5. Storage and security

Music Edit Engine is currently developed as a desktop product with local processing components. Actual storage locations, token protections, any remote data transfers, and retention periods must be confirmed for the version distributed to users. Users should protect access to their device and avoid sharing credentials or authorization files.

6. Disconnecting accounts and deletion requests

Users may request information about personal data handled in connection with Music Edit Engine or seek assistance with deletion by contacting 50018studio@gmail.com. Disconnecting a social account does not itself delete posts hosted on that platform. Available deletion controls depend on the installed application version.

7. Updates

This notice may be updated as Music Edit Engine’s features and integrations evolve. The public policy should always reflect the actual behavior of the current product.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: 50018studio@gmail.com.
